01 · Revenue Cycle Management

$1.2M recovered for a U.S. healthcare provider

Investigated missing charge capture for a U.S. healthcare provider using hypothesis testing, association-rule mining, ML and dashboard-driven pattern discovery.

Outcome

$1.2M in 2025 leakage surfaced and recovered by the client.

Toolkit

Python · ML · Analytics · Power BI · Healthcare

Read the full case study

Situation

A U.S. healthcare provider suspected it was under-billing Medicaid due to gaps in charge capture — charges scattered across EPIC patient records, clinical notes, and medication orders, disconnected from what was actually submitted in claims. With 4M+ claims, manually reviewing each one for missed charges wasn't feasible.

Approach

Started by testing known clinical relationships (e.g., a surgery charge should co-occur with an anesthesia charge on the same day) — accurate, but too slow and too narrow to scale across the full claim volume. Shifted to association-rule mining in Python to let the data surface its own co-billing patterns instead, flagging accounts whose charge combinations deviated from the norm. Led a team of 4 medical coders and 1 Power BI analyst to validate the flagged list against patient records and rebill confirmed cases.

Result

$1.2M in missed 2025 charges identified and recovered, becoming the basis for a follow-on engagement into drug wastage. The pipeline is now being scoped as a productized solution for the client — using ML and agentic AI to automate the hypothesis-testing step itself, rather than relying on manually tested clinical rules.

02 · AI & Forecasting

AI-driven demand forecasting for appeals

Led the development of an AI-driven forecasting tool to predict appeal demand and translate it into staffing, capacity, and budget needs.

Outcome

Replaced manual trend estimates with a live forecasting and capacity-planning dashboard.

Toolkit

GenAI · Python (ML) · Time-Series Forecasting · Fabric · Power BI

Read the full case study

Situation

Appeal volumes — providers challenging denied claims — fluctuate with claim volumes, denial rates, payer mix, and adjudication rule changes, but the business had only historical trends and manual estimates to anticipate them. That made it hard to plan adjudication staffing, workload, and budgets, and left projected savings adjustments uncertain.

Approach

Led a small team — a data engineer and a BI developer — to build an AI-enabled forecasting solution: historical claims and appeal data were cleansed and modeled using time-series methods, backtested to select the most accurate approach, then translated into required adjudication hours and FTE capacity. The output fed a Power BI dashboard, with a generative AI layer added on top so planners could ask plain-language questions like "what's driving the increase in appeals?" and get answers grounded in the validated model, not a black box.

Result

Delivered a forecasting and capacity-planning tool that replaced manual, trend-based guesswork with a data-driven view of future appeal volumes, staffing needs, and financial impact — with a natural-language layer so planners could query drivers and what-if scenarios without needing to interpret the model themselves.

03 · Claims Analytics

Data-backed rule adoption for insurance payers

Analyzed U.S. healthcare insurance claims data to identify cost-saving opportunities and translated findings into interactive BI solutions.

Outcome

Supported initiatives contributing to ~$80M in realized annual client savings.

Toolkit

Hive SQL · Power BI · Tableau · Data Analysis

Read the full case study

Situation

A healthcare claims payment adjudicator processes claims on behalf of U.S. health insurance payers and recommends pay/deny decisions against an exhaustive, constantly evolving proprietary rule engine. Whenever a payer turned on a new rule, denied claims triggered a wave of appeals from hospitals and providers ("provider abrasion"), which made payers reluctant to adopt rules even when they represented real savings, since they had no reliable way to judge whether the payoff was worth the pushback.

Approach

Led the design of a Rule Value Analysis dashboard that quantified a rule's impact before a payer turned it on — projecting potential savings by running the rule against the client's own claims volume, and pairing that with competitive benchmarking on how many other payers had already adopted it, giving clients the confidence to push through expected provider friction. Spent a year-plus as the bridge between the client and a delivery team of 4 data engineers, 6 BI developers, an insights analyst, and go-to-market/sales.

Result

The dashboard became the standard tool payers used to decide which rules to turn on, driving $80M in realized annual savings across the client base.

04 · Automation

From 20+ man-days to under 5 hours

Built an automated workflow for recurring pitch and steering-committee decks, combining Python and Power Automate.

Outcome

Preparation time reduced from 20+ man-days to under five hours.

Toolkit

Python · Power Automate · Automation

Read the full case study

Situation

The provider's financial health rests on numbers that shift by the week — accounts receivable, denials, patient balances — but producing them was a manual assembly line: claims pulled from EPIC, reconciled against data the client sent in Excel, run through financial models by hand, then copied slide by slide into six recurring pitch and steering-committee decks totaling over 100 slides.

Approach

Replaced the assembly line with a pipeline: EPIC data is converted into structured JSON and synced to a cloud SharePoint repository, where a Python script picks it up, runs the same AR, denial, and patient-balance calculations that used to happen in Excel, and writes the results straight into the deck. A consultant still reviews the finished deck and signs off before it goes to the client.

Result

Preparation dropped from 20+ man-days a week to under five hours, freeing the team for analysis instead of slide production.
